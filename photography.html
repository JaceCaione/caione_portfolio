Mossy Log

Taken during a trip to the Kekabeka Falls, this photo displays a moss covered log and the surrounding forest floor; it's been edited to accentuate colder colours and cropped to provide a more focused composition. This project was specifically my 3rd Semester project for my photography class. I had chosen to do more nature photography as I had believed that it was a strongsuit of my previous photos.

Dark Lighting

This photo was taken for a college assignment; we were tasked with photographing food props in dark lighting. The edits that went into the image were to crop out some portions as well as increasing visibility while still keeping the theme. It was an exceptionally interesting experience and served to reinforce my personal preference for calm and desaturated tones, scenes, and colours.
