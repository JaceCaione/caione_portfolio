Texture Work

Made during my 3rd Semester, this piece utilized a premade toad perched on top of a generic orb. Our task for this exercise was to utilize Adobe Substance Painter to create a texture of our choosing. I decided to go for a web-like magma and slate texture, which I believe came out well. My skills with textures, especially procedurally generated materials, have progressed much since this exercise.
